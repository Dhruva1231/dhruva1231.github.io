Flying costs ~$1,025 more and saves ~11.5 hours (~$90 per hour saved).

  • Driving is the cheapest by far: about $200, compared with about $1,225 to fly nonstop and rent a car.
  • The “cheap” flights aren’t worth it. They cost more than driving and save little or no time, and the absolute cheapest is slower than driving.
  • So the real choice is drive or fly Delta nonstop + rental.

The plan

  1. Fri, Nov 20 · travel up
    Dad (Shiva) and Dhira get from Fort Myers to Atlanta, either in the Giulia or on a flight from RSW. Dhruva flies in from SFO (that flight is booked either way and isn’t part of this comparison).
  2. Fri, Nov 20 – Tue, Nov 24 · in Atlanta
    All three of us together. If we flew, we pick up a rental car at ATL; if we drove, we have the Giulia.
  3. Tue, Nov 24 · travel back
    All three head back to Fort Myers the same way Dad and Dhira came up: drive the Giulia home, or fly ATL → RSW.

Left out because they’re the same either way: Dhruva’s SFO flight, the hotel, food, and driving or parking around Atlanta.

Assumptions
  • Door-to-door flight time = flight duration + 2.5 hours for getting to the airport, security, boarding and getting out at the other end.
  • Driving = about 10 hours per leg, including stops.
  • “Travel time” is the total hours spent traveling on the way up plus the way back. It isn’t multiplied by the number of people.
  • Up is 2 people (Dad and Dhira); back is 3 people (Dhruva, Dad and Dhira).
  • Flight prices don’t include bag fees. Frontier charges for carry-ons and checked bags, so options C and D cost more than shown.
  • The hotel, food, Dhruva’s SFO flight and getting around Atlanta cost about the same either way, so they’re left out.
